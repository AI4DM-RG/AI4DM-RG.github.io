AI4Decision Making Reading Group
AI for Decision Making (AI4DM) is an open, collaborative forum dedicated to advancing the science of choosing actions under uncertainty. We host presentations and discussions of papers and case studies at the intersection of probabilistic modeling, optimization, and machine learning for decision support in single- and multi-agent autonomous settings, with attention to robustness, interpretability, safety, and responsible deployment in real systems.
