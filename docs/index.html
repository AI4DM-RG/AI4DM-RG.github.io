AI4DM Reading Group
AI for Decision Making (AI4DM) is a Concordia reading group dedicated to advancing the science of choosing actions under uncertainty. We host presentations and discussions of papers and case studies at the intersection of probabilistic modeling, optimization, operations research, and machine learning.
